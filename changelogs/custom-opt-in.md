title: New custom opt-in on participations
author: Jean-Christophe Camus
date: "2026-01-08"
type: Added
hidden: false
---

The `opt_in` object of the respondent now includes a `custom` boolean, alongside `newsletter`, `email` and `sms`. It tells whether the respondent accepted your custom opt-in: the additional consent checkbox, with your own wording, that can be enabled on your collection widget.
