author: Jean-Christophe Camus
date: "2023-09-13"

hidden: false
---

Participations returned by the Responses API (list and detail) now include an `alert_triggered` boolean, telling whether the participation triggered a dissatisfaction alert.

See [Surveys & Question Types](doc:surveys-question-types) for how alerts are configured with `alert_limit`.
