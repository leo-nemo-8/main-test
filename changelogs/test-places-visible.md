title: Test points of sale available in the API
author: Jean-Christophe Camus
date: "2025-07-04"

hidden: false
---

Test points of sale are now returned by `GET /places` and `GET /places/{id}`, like your other points of sale.

The API does not flag them as test points of sale: if you synchronise your points of sale from the API, ask your Goodays contact for the list of your test points of sale so you can exclude them.
