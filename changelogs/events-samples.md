`GET /events` lists sample payloads for each webhook event, built from your own data, to help you develop and test your webhook endpoint:

- `GET /events/_/sample_response`
- `GET /events/_/sample_reply`
- `GET /events/_/sample_message`
- `GET /events/_/sample_delegated`

See [Converse API](doc:create-your-interface) for details.
