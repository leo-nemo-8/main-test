`GET /stats/satisfaction` now supports CSAT questions. If your surveys include CSAT questions, the customer satisfaction score is calculated on the answers to these questions; otherwise, it is still calculated on star questions.

See [Customer-Satisfaction Score](doc:customer-satisfaction-score) for details.
